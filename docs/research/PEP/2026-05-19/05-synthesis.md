1. **Diversified food + beverage с Frito-Lay leadership**

   - 55% revenue from higher-margin snacks (~25-28% op margin)
   - Volume recovery confirmed Q1 2026 (+2% Frito-Lay)

2. **Valuation premium discount vs KO**
   - Fwd P/E 17.5x vs KO 24.1x (-27%)
   - Yield 3.74% vs KO 2.74% (+100bp)
   - Similar ROIC ~14-15% — premium not justified

3. **Strong dividend story**
   - 52 года подряд повышений — Dividend King top tier
   - 5%+ DPS growth annually
   - Payout 70% sustainable

4. **Volume recovery + improving narrative**
   - Frito-Lay +2% volume в Q1 2026 disconfirms GLP-1 harsh bear scenarios
   - 300M new consumption occasions YoY
   - Innovation pipeline restored

5. **Underperformance = mean-reversion setup**
   - -25% drawdown 12m

   - 14 Holds → upgrade room

## Bear case (5)

1. **Slow growth structurally**
   - Revenue growth slowed +2.2% FY2025 (vs KO +1.9% — similar mature)
   - Volume gains modest (+2%), much driven by pricing comp
   - Mid-single-digit EPS growth path

2. **GLP-1 long-term overhang**
   - Snacks consumption -15-25% potential при mass adoption
   - PEP более exposed чем KO (snacks > beverages для GLP-1 impact)
   - Slow-burn trend, not avoidable

3. **Multiple-decade margin pressure**
   - Beverages volume declining structurally в developed markets
   - Healthy alternatives stealing share
   - Limited pricing power без brand premium

4. **Slightly overvalued**
   - Fair value $120-145, midpoint $130
   - Current $149 = +14% над midpoint
   - DCF base $101 — gap large если bear DCF scenario

5. **Higher leverage чем KO**
   - Net debt / EBITDA 2.5x vs KO 2.0x
   - Less M&A flexibility
   - Payout ratio 70% — tight
