# Synthesis — VZ от 2026-05-19

## Bull case (5)

1. **Highest yield + dividend growth in group**
   - **5.91% yield** — best income среди всех holdings
   - **19 лет** consecutive dividend growth (близко к Dividend Aristocrat status)
   - Payout ~67% — sustainable
   - +4-5% DPS growth annually

2. **Q1 2026 turnaround confirmed**
   - **First positive Q1 postpaid net adds in 13 лет** (+55K)
   - Strongest EPS growth (+7.6%) since 2021
   - FY2026 guidance raised
   - Customer momentum sustainable into FY2026

3. **Frontier acquisition transformative**
   - Closed Jan 20, 2026 (~$20B)
   - Added 7-8M broadband customers
   - Synergies materializing
   - Fiber competitive moat strengthening

4. **Undervalued по DCF**
   - DCF fair $76, current $47 = -38% от fair
   - DDM fair $74
   - Triangulated $53-80 range
   - Yield-driven downside protection

5. **Bond-proxy в risk-off**
   - β 0.5-0.6 — defensive
   - Sensitive к interest rates — benefit от rate cuts
   - Stable subscription revenue
   - Recession-resistant essential service

## Bear case (5)

1. **Slow growth structurally**
   - 5y EPS CAGR ~-3% (declining)
   - Revenue growth 2-3% — barely keeping up с inflation
   - Capital appreciation limited
   - Total return mostly от dividend

2. **High debt $119B**
   - 6.4x debt / net income LTM
   - Frontier added debt
   - BBB+ rating — single notch above junk
   - Vulnerable to rate increases

3. **Competitive pressure intensifying**
   - T-Mobile pricing aggressive
   - AT&T fiber accelerating
   - Satellite direct-to-cell (Starlink) disruption
   - Cable MVNO competition

4. **Capex burden**
   - $17B capex annually
   - 5G deployment ongoing
   - Fiber buildout costs
   - Limited multiple expansion

5. **Multi-year underperformance**
   - 5-year RS -90pp vs SPY
   - Slow grower in growth-led market
   - Opportunity cost real
