## Описание фонда

INDY — single-country emerging markets ETF на India. Tracks Nifty 50 — flagship India benchmark, аналог S&P 500 для США. India = 5-я экономика мира по GDP, prime emerging markets growth story.

## Bull case

1. **India structural growth story** — fastest-growing major economy (+6-7% GDP)
2. **Young demographics** — median age 28, consumer expansion ahead
3. **Manufacturing shift** (China+1 strategy) — Apple, foreign direct investment surging
4. **Digital adoption** — UPI payments, e-commerce penetration
5. **Current correction creates entry point** — YTD -14% reset valuations

## Bear case

1. **Valuation premium structural** — Nifty 50 P/E typically 22-25x vs global avg 15-17x
2. **Currency risk** — USD/INR depreciation drag (1-3% annually)
3. **Concentration risk** — top 10 = 56.7%, очень концентрированный
4. **Regulatory unpredictability** — tax surprises, capital controls
5. **High expense ratio 0.65%** vs broad EM ETF ~0.10-0.15%
