## Описание фонда

CSSMI — flagship **Swiss equity ETF**, торгуется на SIX Swiss Exchange в CHF. Tracks **Swiss Market Index (SMI)** — 20 крупнейших швейцарских blue-chip компаний. Швейцарский рынок — defensive bedrock через **тройную концентрацию в healthcare + consumer staples + financials**: Nestlé, Roche, Novartis = **~48% от фонда** — fund is essentially "buy three Swiss giants + 17 supporting positions".

## Bull case

1. **Quality defensive country exposure** — Switzerland #1 в global stability rankings
2. **CHF safe-haven appreciation** — historically appreciates vs USD over 10y (~1-2% p.a. tailwind для USD investors)
3. **Concentrated на quality** — Nestlé, Novartis, Roche = elite global compounders
4. **Yield 2.6% в CHF** — attractive given Swiss base rates 0.5-1.0%
5. **Hedge для USD-heavy portfolio** — currency diversification

## Bear case

1. **Extreme concentration** — top 3 = 48% (Novartis + Roche + Nestlé), top 6 = 70%
2. **Pharma cliff risks** — Novartis (Entresto LOE 2026-2027), Roche (oncology pipeline)
3. **Nestlé fundamentals weakening** (GLP-1 narrative + slow growth)
4. **CHF over-appreciation risk** — too strong may hurt corporate exports
5. **TER 0.35%** — выше чем SPY 0.09% или SCHD 0.06%
6. **Available на SIX Swiss Exchange** — не на US exchanges → broker requirements
