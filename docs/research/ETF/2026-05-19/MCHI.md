## Описание фонда

MCHI — **наиболее broad China equity ETF** среди US-listed (vs FXI 50 holdings, KWEB internet-only, ASHR onshore A-shares). Включает H-shares (Hong Kong), B-shares (Mainland в USD), N-shares (US-listed Chinese ADRs), Red-chips, P-chips. **Comprehensive single-country exposure** на 2nd крупнейшую economy in world.

## Bull case

1. **Valuation discount** — China P/E 14.9 vs SPY ~21 (40% cheaper)
2. **Stimulus catalyst** — PBoC easing, fiscal stimulus measures
3. **Tencent + Alibaba dominance** — irreplaceable network effects
4. **Diversification из USD-denominated portfolio**
5. **EM allocation requirement** для balanced portfolio

## Bear case

1. **Geopolitical risk** — US-China tariffs, Taiwan tensions, sanctions
2. **Regulatory crackdowns history** — Alibaba 2020, education 2021, gaming periodically
3. **Property sector overhang** — Evergrande / Country Garden default cascade
4. **Concentration risk** — Top 5 = 33% (Tencent 13%, BABA 11%)
5. **Capital outflows** — foreign investor exits
6. **CCP unpredictability** — VIE structure legal risk
