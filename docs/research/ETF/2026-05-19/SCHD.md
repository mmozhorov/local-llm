## Описание фонда

SCHD — **best-in-class US dividend ETF** с минимальным expense ratio. Tracks Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 — отбирает компании по 4 фундаментальным критериям:

1. **10+ years** consecutive dividend payments
2. **Cash flow / total debt** (financial health)
3. **Return on equity** (profitability)
4. **Dividend yield + 5-year dividend growth rate**

Минимальная market cap $500M USD + liquidity filters. **Это quality dividend filter, не yield trap.**

## Bull case

1. **Самый низкий expense ratio** среди dividend ETF (0.06%)
2. **Quality screen** — 10+ year dividend track + financials
3. **Sector tilt** к defensive (staples + healthcare + energy = 55%)
4. **Yield 3.29% + ~8% dividend growth annually** = double-digit yield-on-cost over time
5. **Outperformer FY2025-2026** (+25% YoY) — momentum + rotation tailwind

## Bear case

1. **Sector concentration** — staples, healthcare, energy underperform в growth markets
2. **No tech mega-cap exposure** — нет AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA
3. **Value tilt** не работает в growth-led markets
4. **Index reconstitution risk** — annual rebalance может cut выигравшие позиции
5. **Higher yield = lower growth** trade-off structural
