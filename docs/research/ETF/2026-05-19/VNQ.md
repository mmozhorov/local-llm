## Описание фонда

VNQ — **largest и most liquid US REIT ETF**, broad-based exposure к US commercial real estate. До 2018 tracking pure MSCI US REIT Index; теперь расширен MSCI US IM Real Estate 25/50 — включает не только pure-play REITs но и **специализированные REITs** (data centers, cell towers) + некоторые real estate management companies. **Mandatory pass-through dividend** от REIT structure → high yield 3.98%.

## Bull case

1. **High yield 3.98%** + tax-advantaged structure (qualified REIT dividends)
2. **Rate cut beneficiary** — Fed easing trajectory = REIT multiple expansion
3. **Diversification из equity exposure** — REITs partially uncorrelated
4. **Inflation hedge structural** — physical assets + rental price-pass-through
5. **Data center / cell tower REITs** = AI infrastructure tailwind

## Bear case

1. **Office REITs structural decline** — WFH permanent, vacancy elevated
2. **Higher-for-longer rates** = continued multiple pressure
3. **Commercial real estate distress cycle** — 2024-2026 refinancing wave
4. **Yield not extreme enough** для true bond replacement
5. **Cap rate expansion** ahead если recession
