1. **Diversification из US-heavy portfolio** — geographic + asset class
2. **Yield 3.7-4.5%** outperforms VNQ
3. **Japan property recovery** — BOJ policy normalization, asset rerating
4. **European property cycle bottoming** — rate cuts complete
5. **FX diversification** — non-USD currency basket
6. **Cheaper valuation** vs US REITs structurally (P/B lower internationally)

## Bear case

1. **Multi-year underperformer** vs VNQ (US REIT outperformance)
2. **Currency drag** — strong USD periods hurt returns
3. **Japan demographic headwind** — declining population vs rental demand
4. **China property crisis** — Evergrande / Country Garden contagion risk
5. **European banking exposure** — secondary risk via real estate lending
6. **Liquidity lower** than US REITs
