## Bear case (5)

1. **GLP-1 long-term consumption headwind**
   - Fast food more exposed чем CPG snacks/beverages
   - Academic studies show 10-25% reduction in fast-food spending among GLP-1 users
   - Slow burn structural risk
   - No quick fix

2. **Consumer macro weakness**
   - Low-income consumer pulling back
   - Higher menu prices vs inflation pushback
   - McValue strategy IS the response — но dilutes margins
   - Real wage growth uncertain в 2026-2027

3. **Operationally improving but stock still bearish trend**
   - Death cross active (50d < 200d)
   - 24-month underperformance vs SPY
   - Bear momentum not yet broken
   - Need quarter-over-quarter consistency

4. **Levered balance sheet**
   - Net debt $51B, net debt / EBITDA 3.5x
   - Negative shareholders equity from buybacks
   - BBB+ rating (vs KO A+)
   - Higher refinancing risk in higher-rate environment

5. **Plant-based + alternative competition**
   - Beyond Meat, Impossible Foods slowly eating share
   - Health-focused fast casual (Chipotle, Sweetgreen) taking premium consumer
   - Long-term category-share erosion possible
