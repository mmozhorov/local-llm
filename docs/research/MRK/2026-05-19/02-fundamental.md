**Capital allocation philosophy:**
- **M&A приоритет** (Acceleron $11B 2021, Prometheus $10.8B 2023, Verona ~$10B 2025, возможно Terns)
- **Dividends sustained** (~$8B annually, 4-5% DPS growth)
- **Buybacks символические** ($0.5-1B annually) — не приоритет
- **Cost savings programme**: ~$3B annual savings к 2027, reinvested в growth

**Payout ratio:** dividend $8B vs FCF $18B = **44%** — sustainable, room для рост.

### Pipeline-funded model

FCF $18B annually обеспечивает:
- $8B dividends
- $1B buybacks
- $5-10B M&A activity / IPR&D licensing
- $1-2B debt paydown / cash build

Это **критически важный fund-flow** для replacement strategy до cliff.

## Вывод по фазе 2

**Quality: AAA-tier** (ROIC, ROE, margins все best-in-class).

**Health: Strong** — A-rated, capacity для M&A.

**Growth: Lumpy** на GAAP (IPR&D charges), но **underlying normalized EPS CAGR 5-6%** + pipeline-replacement option value.

**Valuation: Fair value $100-125, midpoint $110-111.** Current $112 = **roughly fairly valued**.

**Ключевой риск:** **DCF range очень wide ($67 bear / $109 base / $161 bull)** — это **defining feature** MRK: вся история строится вокруг исхода 2028 cliff и pipeline replacement.

**При hold long с +10% — позиция rational:**
- Не overvalued (как KO), не undervalued (как BMY/PFE)
- Asymmetric upside от pipeline (sac-TMT, subcutaneous QLEX) — **bullish скрытая option**
- Asymmetric downside от cliff если pipeline disappointed — **bearish скрытый risk**

**Phase 2 finding:** central decision не на valuation level (fair), а на **conviction в pipeline replacement story**. Это и определит phase 6 action plan.
