# Synthesis — MRK от 2026-05-19

## Bull case

1. **AAA-quality бизнес и top-tier pipeline R&D**
   - ROIC FY2024 21.1% (TTM 22.4%), ROE 36.9%, operating margin non-GAAP 42-45% — best-in-class в big pharma
   - R&D $17.9B annually — один из самых высоких в индустрии
   - Wide moat через intangibles (Keytruda IP, 40+ indications, regulatory expertise)
   - Strong balance sheet: A+ rated, net debt $26B (1.0× EBITDA), capacity для $20-30B M&A

2. **Pipeline replacement story для Keytruda LoE 2028**
   - **Subcutaneous Keytruda QLEX**: EC approved Apr 2026, FDA H2 2026 → extends sc IP до **2042** (14-year hedge)
   - **Sac-TMT (TROP2 ADC)**: 17 Ph3 trials, FDA Priority Review Voucher, potential blockbuster $5-10B peak
   - **Winrevair (PAH)**: $1.44B FY2025 (+244%), $5-8B peak потенциал
   - **Capvaxive / Vaxneuvance**: $1.5B+ combined, ramping
   - **Verona ensifentrine (COPD)**: $2-4B peak добавляет
   - **TERN-701 (CML)**: just acquired (May 2026, $10B), Breakthrough Designation

3. **Setup для bounce на рынке (technical)**
   - **RSI 32** (oversold) — contrarian buy zone
   - Цена на -11% от ATH, в multi-MA support zone ($105-107)
   - Pharma sector defensive rotation тренд может вернуться
   - Strong 12m return +48% подтверждает structural bullish

4. **Sentiment с asymmetric upside**
   - Mean target **$125-130** — **+13-17% upside** vs current
   - **61% buy + 39% hold** = healthy non-crowded (vs KO 85% buy)
   - Options P/C 0.70 — bullish позиционирование
   - Less consensus = room для upgrade при positive surprise

5. **Cliff может быть overstated**
   - Subcutaneous QLEX adoption extends Keytruda exclusivity для большой части indication base
   - Method-of-use, formulation, dosing patents layered → biosimilar entry "death by a thousand cuts" — gradual erosion, не cliff
   - ABBV / Humira parallel: erosion -15-20% Y1, -10% Y2, не катастрофа
   - Pipeline density (Sac-TMT + Verona + Terns + Winrevair) impressive в diversification

## Bear case

1. **Keytruda concentration риск (49% revenue)**
   - **$31.7B** revenue от одного продукта в pharma compositional patent expiry в 2028
   - Историческая cliff-erosion: PFE Lipitor -50% за 18 мес, ABBV Humira -36% за 24 мес → Keytruda может потерять $10-15B annual revenue в 2029-2030
   - Pipeline replacement требует **everything to go right** — низкая probability полного offset
   - Bear DCF case fair value **$67** — 40% downside

2. **IRA Round 2 (Jan 2027) — Keytruda price negotiation**
   - Effective Jan 1, 2027 — внутри scope горизонта
   - Negotiated price на 20-40% ниже list для Medicare population
   - Estimated revenue impact: **-2 to -4% annually, growing**
   - Konkretny катализатор, не speculation

3. **Gardasil structural decline (China + Japan + HHS)**
   - $8.6B → $5.2B FY2025 (-39%) — vertical drop
   - China inventory destocking + local competition (Innovent, Walvax)
   - Japan national catchup program завершен
   - HHS / RFK Jr. vaccine skepticism US — political headwind
   - Менеджмент **withdrew $11B sales target** — credibility hit

4. **EPS volatility от serial M&A**
   - 2023 Prometheus IPR&D charge → EPS $1.51 (vs $7.48)
   - 2026 Verona + Terns IPR&D charges → **EPS depressed to $5.04-5.16**
   - Pattern continues — каждый второй year is "noisy"
   - Multiple compression от accounting volatility (даже если cash flow клин)

5. **No anchor shareholder**
   - Insider ownership всего 0.22%
   - **No Buffett-equivalent** structural buyer-of-last-resort
   - Это структурный минус — больше vulnerability к sentiment swings

6. **Technical pullback может ускориться**
   - **Death cross** risk (50d-MA $120 близок к пробитию ниже 200d-MA $118)
   - При пробое $105-107 support cluster — open до $97, далее $90
   - Max drawdown 12m уже -26% — в scope drawdown limit (20%) попадает
