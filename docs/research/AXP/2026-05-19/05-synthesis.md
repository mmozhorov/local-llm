# Synthesis — AXP от 2026-05-19

## Bull case (5)

1. **Premium closed-loop network + lending hybrid**
   - Unique business model — direct merchant relationships + own credit
   - Higher economics per cardmember чем V/MA pure-network
   - Premium customer base resilient ($30K avg spend vs $5K V/MA)
   - ROE 33-36% — best-in-class financials

2. **Durable card fee growth — 30 кварталов double-digit**
   - $10B record FY2025 card fees (+18%)
   - Subscription-like income stream
   - Pricing power proven (Platinum fee multi раз повышалась)

3. **Berkshire 21% structural anchor**
   - Один из самых концентрированных Buffett holdings (наравне с KO)
   - Held since 1991 — never sold
   - **Structural downside protection** — самый сильный среди всех holdings

4. **Q1 2026 fundamental excellence**
   - Revenue +11%, EPS +18%, billed business +10% (highest 3y)
   - ROE 36.6% — record territory
   - FY2026 guidance reaffirmed, FX tailwind

5. **Valuation discount setup**
   - Fwd P/E 17.6x на $17.60 EPS
   - vs V/MA at 28-32x — significant discount
   - vs LT AXP median 17-19x — нижний конец range
   - **Fair value $300-410, midpoint $365** = +18% upside

## Bear case (5)

1. **Procyclical exposure**
   - β 1.2 — выше market sensitivity
   - Recession would hit spending volumes hard
   - Premium consumer concentration двойной risk (high spending, but slowdown vulnerable)

2. **Credit cycle worsening**
   - Net write-off rate +20bp YoY to 2.0% в FY2024
   - Provisions $5.2B FY2024
   - Trajectory concerning if accelerate в 2026-2027

3. **Stock at scope drawdown limit**
   - 12m drawdown -27%, current -20% от ATH
   - User scope 20% limit под угрозой
   - Tight risk management mandatory

4. **Higher rate environment headwinds**
   - Consumer credit demand can soften
   - Refi cost от business borrowers
   - Funding cost pressure (но less than pure banks)

5. **Digital disruption LT**
   - BNPL (Affirm, Klarna) attacking younger demographic
   - Apple Pay / Google Pay могут disintermediate
   - Crypto / stablecoins evolving payment rails
   - Slow burn structural
