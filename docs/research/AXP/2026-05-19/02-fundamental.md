## DCF / DDM

### Financial firms: use DDM + multiples

**Dividend Discount Model:**
- DPS FY2025: $3.80 (annual)
- Growth: 12% (5y avg)
- Cost of equity: 9% (β 1.2, equity premium 6%)
- DDM fair value: $3.80 × 1.12 / (0.09 - 0.04 LT g) = **$85** на conservative growth assumption — too low

Better: **Multi-stage DDM:**
- Stage 1 (Y1-5): 12% DPS growth (recent rate)
- Stage 2 (Y6-10): 8%
- Stage 3 LT: 4%
- Y1 DPS: $4.26
- Sum PV stages: complex calc, approximate **fair value $260-320** на reasonable assumptions
