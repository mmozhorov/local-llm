# Context — PG от 2026-05-19

## Бизнес в одном абзаце

Procter & Gamble — **#1 global consumer household products company** (~$84B revenue), портфель из ~65 ведущих брендов в 5 категориях: Fabric Care + Home Care (Tide, Downy, Mr. Clean), Baby/Feminine/Family Care (Pampers, Always, Bounty, Charmin), Beauty (Olay, Pantene, Head & Shoulders, SK-II), Grooming (Gillette, Venus, Braun), Health Care (Crest, Oral-B, Vicks, Pepto-Bismol). Операционная модель: **branded CPG с pricing power** + scale advantages в R&D + global distribution + scale-driven marketing efficiency. **Длиннейший dividend record S&P 500 (135 лет payment, 70 лет growth)** — quintessential Dividend King. Stable mid-single-digit organic growth с margin expansion engineering. Slow grower, но **highest-quality consumer staples bedrock**.

## Moat

- **Wide moat 5/5** через **intangible brand assets** (Tide, Pampers, Gillette — category leaders), **scale advantages** (R&D, marketing efficiency, supply chain), **switching costs** (consumer preference inertia)
- **70 лет dividend growth + 135 лет payments** = бренд reliability
- **Прочность LT 5/5** — самый структурно сильный moat среди staples
- Главные LT угрозы: private label penetration (slow burn), Chinese / EM local competitors, e-commerce direct brands
