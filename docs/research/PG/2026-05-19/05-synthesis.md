# Synthesis — PG от 2026-05-19

## Bull case (5)

1. **Highest-quality consumer staples**
   - ROIC 18-22% — top tier
   - Operating margin 25.6% — solid premium
   - Wide moat 5/5 через 65+ brand portfolio
   - Adjusted FCF productivity 87% — best in class

2. **Best balance sheet в peer group**
   - Net debt $25.8B / EBITDA 1.2x — lowest leverage
   - AA-/Aa3 credit rating — highest в группе
   - Interest coverage ~20x — excellent
   - Strong M&A capacity

3. **70 лет dividend growth — top-tier Dividend King**
   - 135 лет dividend payments continuous
   - Reliability premium structural
   - ~3% yield + 5%/yr DPS growth = double-digit yield-on-cost over 10 years

4. **Fairly valued**
   - DCF fair $145, current $146 ≈ exact match
   - Triangulated $135-170, midpoint $150
   - LT median P/E 22-24x, current 21x — slightly cheap
   - Limited downside от valuation

5. **9 of 10 categories growing organically**
   - Broad-based strength
   - Volume flat (Q1 FY2026) — stable, not declining
   - Pricing power intact
   - Diversified across staples

## Bear case (5)

1. **Slow growth structurally**
   - 5y EPS CAGR ~4%, revenue ~2-3%
   - Volume growth marginal
   - Multiple expansion limited
   - Vs growth-led market = opportunity cost

2. **Multi-year underperformer vs market**
   - 5-year RS -45pp vs SPY
   - Capital appreciation lag persistent
   - Investor flight from staples в growth periods
   - Recovery requires defensive rotation

3. **China beauty weakness**
   - SK-II segment underperforming
   - Local competitors gaining share
   - Premium beauty consumption pressured
   - Drag on Beauty segment growth

4. **FX headwind potential**
   - Global ex-US exposure significant
   - USD strength может hit revenue
   - Reported numbers volatile

5. **Multiple compression risk**
   - At 21x fwd P/E, room для compression к 18x = -14%
   - Macro rotation away от premium staples
   - Yield not extreme enough to attract income flow
