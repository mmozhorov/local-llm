   - $397B cash (Treasuries earning ~5% = $20B annually)
   - $176B insurance float (no-cost leverage)
   - AA+/Aa2 credit (highest corporate)
   - Massive optionality для major deployment

2. **Diversified quality compounder**
   - Operating businesses: GEICO, BNSF, BHE — top-tier moats
   - Investment portfolio $263B concentrated на quality (AAPL, AXP, KO, BAC, GOOGL, CVX)
   - 60-year track record of capital allocation excellence
   - Operating earnings $44.5B FY2025 stable

3. **Attractive valuation**
   - P/B 1.44 — historically attractive (LT median 1.5-1.6x)
   - SoTP fair value $560-680, midpoint $610 = **+25% upside**
   - At Buffett buyback threshold 1.2x within 17% downside protection

4. **CEO transition smooth**
   - Greg Abel — 25+ years BHE CEO, Vice Chairman since 2018
   - Buffett endorsement public
   - First quarter strong operating earnings +18%
   - First major bet (GOOGL +225%) signals modern thinking

5. **Defensive characteristics**
   - β 0.85 — lower volatility
   - Low drawdown -10% от ATH
   - Cash buffer protects in market corrections
   - No dividend obligation pressure

## Bear case (5)

1. **Buffett intangible decay risk**
   - "Buffett premium" might erode over time without active management
   - Conglomerate discount может вернуться
   - Concerns about Abel's track record outside BHE

2. **Cash drag on returns**
   - $397B yielding ~5% LT = ~$20B annually
   - But limits compound rate vs all-equity portfolio
   - If Abel doesn't deploy at >7% returns, drag continues

3. **Q1 2026 first quarter miss**
   - Operating earnings $11.35B vs $11.56B consensus
   - Initial signal of execution challenges
   - Watch quarterly cadence for confirmation

4. **Concentration risk in portfolio**
   - AAPL 22% — single name exposure
   - Top 5 holdings = 67% of portfolio
   - Any single-name shock matters

5. **Recession risk**
   - BNSF rail volumes cyclical
   - BHE utility regulated but capex-heavy
   - Insurance underwriting cyclical
   - Diversification limits but doesn't eliminate cycle exposure
