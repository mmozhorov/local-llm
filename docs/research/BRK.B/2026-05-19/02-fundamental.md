## Вырез risks

1. **Buffett intangible transition risk** — Greg Abel опытен (BHE 25+ years), но "Buffett premium" может decay
2. **Cash drag** — $397B yielding ~5% short-term, but limits compound rate
3. **Capital deployment failure** — if Abel can't deploy at >7% returns, performance suffers
4. **Conglomerate discount** — может вернуться post-Buffett

## Вывод

- **Quality: AAA-tier diversified** — unique in S&P 500

- **Valuation: Undervalued** (fair $560-680, midpoint $610; current $485 = **-20% от midpoint**)
